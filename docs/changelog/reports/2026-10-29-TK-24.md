# Report: TK-24 web, organizations, members and project creation (2026-10-29)

## Summary
`apps/web` gains three screens on top of the TK-23 procedures: create or choose an organization (`/organizations`),
members and invitation (`/members`), and project creation (panel on `/projects`). A web guard based on the session
(`hasSession`, `hasActiveOrganization`) removes the `/login` <-> `/projects` loop for a session without organization.
No change in `apps/api`, `packages/contract` or the database.
