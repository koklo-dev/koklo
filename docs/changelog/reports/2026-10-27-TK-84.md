# Period report — 2026-10-27 (TK-84, UI components and icons)

components of DESIGN.md §5, `IconButton` and `StatusBadge`, all through its entry point only.

See [architecture.md](../../architecture.md#ui-components-and-icons-tk-84) and
[ADR 0005](../../adr/0005-ui-components-icons-radix.md). Icons are generated modules under
