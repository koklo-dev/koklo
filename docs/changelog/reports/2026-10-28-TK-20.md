# Report: TK-20 multi-tenant Prisma extension (2026-10-28)

## Summary
`@koklo/db` filters business models (`Project`) by organization through a Prisma `$extends`
and `withOrganization` (AsyncLocalStorage). No context, a foreign organization or raw SQL
on the extended client raise named errors.

## Architecture and Runtime
`createRawDbClient` vs `createDbClient`; api `DatabaseService.raw` / `client` on one pool.
See [architecture.md](../../architecture.md#multi-tenant-isolation-tk-20).

## Quality Gates and Fixes
New models with `organizationId` must be classified (guard test), see
[quality-and-operations.md](../../quality-and-operations.md#multi-tenant-isolation-tk-20).

## Risks and Follow-ups
- `upsert` ownership check is not atomic; the unique constraint is the safety net.
- Raw client misuse is a convention (documented), not enforced by types.
- Role-based authorization (TK-23) and RLS remain out of scope.
