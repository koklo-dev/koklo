# Sign in and move around the application

**Who this is for**: anyone with a GitHub account allowed to use Koklo. Some entries depend on your role: a client sees only the
pages their projects open to them.
**Before you start**: the address of the application.
**Result**: you are signed in, you know the side menu, you can jump to any project, feature or ticket with the search (Ctrl K),
and you can fold the menu or open it as a drawer on a phone.

## Steps

1. Open the application. On the "Connexion à Koklo" page, click **Continuer avec GitHub**, then authorise the sign-in on
   GitHub. You arrive on your last project, or on **Projets** if you have none.
2. The side menu has three parts. Under **PROJET** (only on a project page) you find the project card with its phase,
   **Vue d'ensemble**, **Idée**, the **SPEC** group and **Tickets**. Under **ORGANISATION** you find
   **Tous les projets** and **Réglages** (members, GitHub, AI), shown only if your role allows it.
3. The **SPEC** group lists the seven deliverables in the order they are written, with a counter (for example "0/7"). An entry
   with a padlock is not available yet: put the focus on it to read when it opens (for example "Disponible après les
   données"). Entries that exist but are not delivered yet open a page that says **Bientôt**.
4. To change project, click the project card: it lists your eight most recent projects, **Tous les projets** and, for owners and
   administrators, **Créer un projet**.
5. To find something, press **Ctrl K** (**⌘ K** on a Mac) from any page, even while typing in a field, or click **Rechercher**.
   Type a few letters: the pages of the menu appear at once, then projects, features and tickets of the current project
   about 0.2 seconds after you stop typing. At most 20 results are shown, with the total when there are more
   ("134 résultats · 20 affichés"). Use the arrow keys and **Entrée** (or click) to open a result; **Échap** closes the search and
   puts you back where you were. Clients never see features in the results.
6. To fold the menu to icons, use **Replier la barre** at the bottom of the menu, or Ctrl+B (Cmd+B on Mac). Folded, hover or
   focus an icon to see its name. The menu stays folded the next time you come back.
7. At the bottom of the menu, the user menu (**Menu utilisateur**) offers **Se déconnecter**, and the moon or sun button switches
   between light and dark. On a low screen only the middle of the menu scrolls: the fold, user and theme buttons stay in place.
8. On a phone (narrow screen) the menu is hidden: press **Ouvrir le menu** to open it as a drawer; Escape closes it and brings
   you back to the button.

Screenshots (light, dark and 375 px) are generated by the end-to-end tests into `design/screens/captures/S-app-shell/`
(expanded and folded menu, SPEC group, padlock tooltip, drawer, headers, "Bientôt" pages, search states).

## Checking it worked
The menu shows the page you are on with a highlighted entry, the page title appears under a line that reads
"Project / Section / Page", and there is no horizontal scrolling on a phone. Ctrl K opens a search box named "Recherche".

## If something goes wrong
| Symptom | Cause | What to do |
|---------|-------|------------|
| A red message appears on the login page | GitHub sign-in failed | Click **Continuer avec GitHub** again |
| The menu is not visible on a phone | It is a drawer | Press **Ouvrir le menu** |
| An entry of the SPEC group has a padlock | The deliverable it follows is not ready | Read the condition shown on focus; it opens by itself |
| "La recherche a échoué. Réessayez." | The search could not be reached | Press **Réessayer** |
| "Réglages" is missing | Your role does not manage members | Ask an owner or administrator |
| An old address (`/kanban`, `/members`, `/github`) | Pages moved | You are redirected; update the bookmark |
