# Sign in and move around the application

**Who this is for**: anyone with a GitHub account allowed to use Koklo.
**Before you start**: the address of the application.
**Result**: you are signed in, you see the "Projets" list and you can fold or open the side menu.

## Steps

1. Open the application. On the "Connexion à Koklo" page, click **Continuer avec GitHub**.
   The page is light by default. The theme button at the top right switches to dark.
2. Authorise the sign-in on GitHub. You arrive on **Projets**.
3. In the side menu, "Projets" is the current page. Use the button at the top of the page
   (**Réduire la barre latérale** / **Ouvrir la barre latérale**), or Ctrl+B (Cmd+B on Mac),
   to fold the menu to icons or open it again. Folded, hover or focus an icon to see its name.
4. To change theme, use **Passer au thème sombre** / **Passer au thème clair** at the bottom of the menu.
5. On a phone (narrow screen) the menu is hidden: press **Ouvrir la barre latérale** to open it
   as a drawer; Escape closes it.

Screenshots (light and dark): not captured yet — the capture needs the application running with
a signed-in session.

## Checking it worked
The "Projets" title and your projects table appear, with no horizontal scrolling on a phone.

## If something goes wrong
| Symptom | Cause | What to do |
|---------|-------|------------|
| A red message appears on the login page | GitHub sign-in failed | Click **Continuer avec GitHub** again |
| The menu is not visible on a phone | It is a drawer | Press **Ouvrir la barre latérale** |
